{/* Shared flow (D16): create a DSM app that authenticates with a certificate
    from a trusted CA, such as a CCM zone CA.
    Props: name (default model-server-access); group (default model-keys). */}

<Walkthrough label="Create a Trusted CA app">
<Step
  title="Open Apps"
  image={require('../img/dsm-apps-2.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[94.2, 16.1, 4.9, 5.0]]}
  alt="The Apps page in DSM, with ADD APP outlined">

In DSM, go to **Apps** and click **ADD APP**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Name the app and pick Trusted CA"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-trusted-ca.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[14.2, 29.5, 42.0, 7.2], [56.8, 30.8, 25.5, 5.9], [29.8, 54.3, 7.9, 6.0]]}
  alt="The new app form, with the app name, the interface and Trusted CA outlined">

Enter the **App name**, such as <code>{props.name ?? 'model-server-access'}</code>, set the interface to **REST API**, and under **Authentication method**, select **Trusted CA**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Enter the DNS name and the CA"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-san-ca.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[14.5, 31.1, 56.1, 7.9], [14.5, 45.7, 82.7, 54.0]]}
  alt="The SAN and trusted CA sections, with the DNS name and the CA certificate outlined">

Under **Configure Subject Alternative Name (SAN)**, keep **DNS Name** and enter <code>{'<APPLICATION_ID>.app.fortanix.cloud'}</code>. Under **UPLOAD TRUSTED CA CERT**, upload the zone CA certificate, or paste it into the box.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Assign the group and save"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-groups-2.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[57.4, 13.2, 33.2, 8.4], [92.6, 82.0, 4.7, 5.0]]}
  alt="The groups the app is assigned to, with the group and SAVE outlined">

Scroll to **Assigning the new app to groups** and click the group, such as <code>{props.group ?? 'model-keys'}</code>, so that it moves to the list on the right. Click **SAVE**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Note the app's UUID"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-access-page.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[13.3, 21.0, 21.6, 5.4]]}
  alt="The new app, with its UUID outlined">

The app opens. Note its **UUID**. The workload's InitData carries it.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Check the DNS name"
  image={require('../img/dsm-trusted-ca-view.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[26.9, 31.6, 45.4, 7.9]]}
  alt="The View Trusted CA dialog, with the DNS name outlined">

To check the app, click **VIEW TRUSTED CA**. The **DNS Name** is the application's ID under <code>app.fortanix.cloud</code>.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
