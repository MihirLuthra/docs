{/* Shared flow (D16): create a DSM app that authenticates with a certificate
    from a trusted CA, such as a CCM zone CA.
    Props: name (default model-server-access); group (default model-keys). */}

<Walkthrough label="Create a Trusted CA app">
<Step
  title="Open Apps"
  image={require('../img/dsm-apps.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={501}
  marks={[[94.0, 20.8, 5.4, 8.4]]}
  alt="The Apps page in DSM, with ADD APP outlined">

In DSM, go to **Apps** and click **ADD APP**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Set Trusted CA authentication"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-trusted-ca.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={706}
  marks={[[19.2, 6.9, 9.9, 8.2], [1.7, 40.5, 65.1, 10.2], [1.7, 56.2, 95.5, 43.8]]}
  alt="The new app form on Trusted CA, with the method, the DNS name and the CA certificate outlined">

Enter the **App name**, such as <code>{props.name ?? 'model-server-access'}</code>, and set **Interface** to **REST API**. Under **Authentication method**, select **Trusted CA**. Under **Configure Subject Alternative Name (SAN)**, keep **DNS Name** and enter <code>{'<APPLICATION_ID>.app.fortanix.cloud'}</code>. Under **UPLOAD TRUSTED CA CERT**, upload the zone CA certificate, or paste it into the box.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Assign the group and save"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-groups.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={648}
  marks={[[50.5, 8.8, 46.6, 12.5], [91.2, 90.4, 6.0, 7.7]]}
  alt="The groups the app is assigned to, with the group and SAVE outlined">

Under **Assigning the new app to groups**, click the group, such as <code>{props.group ?? 'model-keys'}</code>, so that it moves to the list on the right. Click **SAVE**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Note the app's UUID"
  image={require('../img/dsm-app-workload-page.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={711}
  marks={[[0.5, 12.9, 25.5, 5.8]]}
  alt="The new app, with its UUID outlined">

The app opens. Note its **UUID**. The workload's InitData carries it.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Check the DNS name"
  image={require('../img/dsm-trusted-ca-view.webp').default}
  width={932}
  height={441}
  marks={[[1.8, 46.0, 94.6, 16.6]]}
  alt="The View Trusted CA dialog, with the DNS name outlined">

To check the app, click **VIEW TRUSTED CA**. The **DNS Name** is the application's name under <code>app.fortanix.cloud</code>.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
