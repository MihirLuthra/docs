{/* Shared flow (D16): link a publisher's group and a consumer's group in two
    CCM accounts (collaborating groups). Groups are in Armor's Identity &
    Access Management. */}

<Walkthrough label="Link the groups">
<Step
  title="Publisher. Open the group"
  image={require('../img/iam-group-page.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={697}
  marks={[[83.2, 18.9, 7.7, 6.6]]}
  alt="The group page in Identity & Access Management, with COLLABORATE outlined">

In Armor's **Identity & Access Management**, go to **Groups**, open the group, and click **COLLABORATE**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Generate and copy the token"
  image={require('../img/iam-collaborate.webp').default}
  width={875}
  height={565}
  marks={[[3.2, 56.1, 93.5, 13.5], [3.2, 70.3, 6.9, 7.3]]}
  alt="The Collaborate dialog, with the token and COPY outlined">

Click **GENERATE TOKEN**, then **COPY**. Send the token to the consumer over a channel you trust.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Open the group"
  image={require('../img/iam-group-page.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={697}
  marks={[[91.5, 18.9, 7.9, 6.6]]}
  alt="A group page in Identity & Access Management, with ACCEPT TOKEN outlined">

In the consumer's account, open its group in **Identity & Access Management** and click **ACCEPT TOKEN**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Paste the token"
  image={require('../img/iam-accept-token.webp').default}
  width={874}
  height={377}
  marks={[[3.1, 62.9, 93.7, 12.2], [3.1, 77.5, 12.8, 12.5]]}
  alt="The Accept token dialog, with the token field and PROCEED outlined">

Paste the token under **Paste token** and click **PROCEED**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Accept the link"
  image={require('../img/iam-collab-accept.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={685}
  marks={[[22.5, 42.6, 8.9, 8.6], [82.4, 77.8, 13.6, 8.2]]}
  alt="Collaboration Groups on PUBLISHER, with the row menu open and Accept outlined">

Go to **COLLABORATION GROUPS** > **PUBLISHER**. The consumer's group is **Pending**. In the row's menu, click **Accept**. The link is now **Accepted**, and the consumer sees it under **CONSUMER**.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
