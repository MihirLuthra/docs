{/* Shared flow (D16): link a publisher's group and a consumer's group in two
    CCM accounts (collaborating groups). Groups are in Armor's Identity &
    Access Management. */}

<Walkthrough label="Link the groups">
<Step
  title="Publisher. Open the group"
  image={require('../img/iam-group-created.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[13.6, 42.4, 85.4, 7.4]]}
  alt="The publisher's Groups page in Identity & Access Management, with the group outlined">

In the publisher's account, open **Identity & Access Management** from the account's home page, go to **Groups**, and click the group.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Click Collaborate"
  image={require('../img/iam-group-page.webp').default}
  width={1548}
  height={784}
  marks={[[85.8, 11.6, 6.3, 4.0]]}
  alt="The publisher's group page, with COLLABORATE outlined">

On the group's page, click **COLLABORATE**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Generate and copy the token"
  image={require('../img/iam-collaborate.webp').default}
  width={1548}
  height={784}
  marks={[[31.8, 51.5, 36.2, 9.6], [31.8, 61.6, 2.7, 3.7]]}
  alt="The Collaborate dialog, with the token and COPY outlined">

Click **GENERATE TOKEN**, then **COPY**. Send the token to the consumer over a channel you trust.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Click Accept token"
  image={require('../img/iam-group-page-consumer.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[91.8, 16.4, 7.3, 5.1]]}
  alt="The consumer's group page, with ACCEPT TOKEN outlined">

In the consumer's account, open its group the same way, under **Identity & Access Management** > **Groups**, and click **ACCEPT TOKEN**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Paste the token"
  image={require('../img/iam-accept-token.webp').default}
  width={1548}
  height={784}
  marks={[[31.8, 51.6, 36.2, 6.2], [31.8, 59.3, 4.6, 3.8]]}
  alt="The Accept token dialog, with the token field and PROCEED outlined">

Paste the token under **Paste token** and click **PROCEED**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Accept the link"
  image={require('../img/iam-collab-accept.webp').default}
  width={1548}
  height={784}
  marks={[[19.2, 26.2, 8.1, 5.4], [83.7, 47.4, 11.0, 4.3]]}
  alt="Collaboration Groups on PUBLISHER, with the tab and Accept in the row menu outlined">

In the publisher's account, go to **Groups** > **COLLABORATION GROUPS** > **PUBLISHER**. The consumer's group is **Pending**. In the row's menu, click **Accept**. The link is now **Accepted**, and the consumer sees it under **CONSUMER**.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
