<Walkthrough label="Create and approve the shared workflow">
<Step
  title="Consumer. Name the workflow"
  image={require('../img/ccm-workflow-add-consumer.webp').default}
  width={1548}
  height={784}
  marks={[[31.2, 42.6, 18.6, 5.4], [49.6, 42.6, 18.4, 5.4], [36.0, 58.3, 7.3, 3.8]]}
  alt="The Add Workflow dialog, with the name, the group and ADD WORKFLOW outlined">

In the consumer's account, go to **Workflows** in CCM and click **ADD WORKFLOW**. Enter a **Name**, such as `model-server-shared`, select the group, and click **ADD WORKFLOW**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Add a placeholder for the publisher's application"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-placeholder.webp').default}
  width={1513}
  height={758}
  marks={[[11.1, 32.7, 6.5, 5.0], [11.1, 43.4, 10.6, 5.0], [1.8, 54.0, 96.7, 5.5], [8.5, 91.7, 11.4, 6.1]]}
  alt="The application picker on Groups and Publisher group, with the group and ADD APPLICATION outlined">

Drag the **Application** icon onto the graph and click **ADD APPLICATION** on the node. Under **View by**, select **Groups**, then **Publisher group**, select the publisher's group, and click **ADD APPLICATION**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Save the draft"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-consumer-draft.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={778}
  marks={[[44.6, 68.0, 11.1, 8.7], [76.0, 16.7, 7.1, 6.4]]}
  alt="The draft with the placeholder node and SAVE DRAFT outlined">

The node is a placeholder for the publisher's application. Click **SAVE DRAFT**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Open the shared draft"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-shared-draft.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={550}
  marks={[[33.2, 52.2, 7.6, 11.5], [91.2, 76.7, 6.0, 8.0]]}
  alt="The publisher's Draft workflows, with the shared workflow's VIEW DETAILS outlined">

In the publisher's account, go to **Workflows** > **Draft**. The workflow is marked **Shared**. Click **VIEW DETAILS**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Fill in the placeholder"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-fill-placeholder.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={550}
  marks={[[15.7, 60.5, 11.3, 12.4], [92.5, 24.0, 6.8, 8.4]]}
  alt="The shared draft, with the placeholder and SAVE DRAFT outlined">

Click **ADD APPLICATION** on the placeholder, select the application, its build and its configuration, and click **ADD APPLICATION**. Click **SAVE DRAFT**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Request approval"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-request.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[41.5, 57.9, 9.4, 5.0]]}
  alt="The confirmation dialog, with REQUEST APPROVAL outlined">

In the consumer's account, open the draft, click **SAVE AND REQUEST APPROVAL**, and confirm with **REQUEST APPROVAL**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Publisher. Approve"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-pending-shared.webp').default}
  width={1903}
  height={550}
  marks={[[21.8, 52.2, 12.3, 11.5], [14.7, 74.0, 84.5, 12.9]]}
  alt="The publisher's Pending Approval tab, with the shared workflow outlined">

In the publisher's account, go to **Workflows** > **Pending Approval**, click the workflow, check the graph, and click **APPROVE**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Consumer. Approve"
  image={require('../img/ccm-wf-approve.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[19.6, 25.6, 60.1, 41.4], [24.9, 82.7, 5.3, 4.6]]}
  alt="The approval request, with APPROVE outlined">

In the consumer's account, go to **Workflows** > **Pending Approval**, click the workflow, and click **APPROVE**. It moves to **Approved**, in both accounts.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
