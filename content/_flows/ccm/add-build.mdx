    Props: runtime, "cc" (default) or "eos". */}

<Walkthrough label="Add a build">
<Step
  title="Open the application"
  image={require('../img/ccm-app-page.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[92.6, 49.6, 4.8, 4.4]]}
  alt="The application page on BUILDS, with the ADD BUILD button outlined">

Go to **Applications** and open the application. On **BUILDS**, click **ADD BUILD**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Name the build"
  image={props.runtime === 'eos' ? require('../img/ccm-build-version-eos.webp').default : require('../img/ccm-build-version.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[28.4, 30.1, 41.5, 7.5]]}
  alt="The Add Build dialog, with the Build version field outlined">

Enter a unique **Build version**, such as `1.0`.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Enter the measurements"
  image={props.runtime === 'eos' ? require('../img/ccm-build-measurements-eos.webp').default : require('../img/ccm-build-measurements.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={props.runtime === 'eos' ? [[28.4, 18.0, 41.5, 44.0], [28.4, 74.6, 11.1, 6.6], [33.8, 86.2, 6.0, 4.4]] : [[28.4, 13.8, 41.5, 53.2], [28.4, 79.4, 11.1, 6.3], [33.8, 86.4, 6.0, 4.4]]}
  alt="The measurements, the NVIDIA GPU attestation setting and the ADD BUILD button outlined">

Scroll down and enter the measurements: MRTD and RTMR0 to RTMR3{props.runtime === 'eos' ? ', from the converter' : ', and MR_CONFIG_ID, the hash of the InitData'}. Under **Coprocessors**, set **NVIDIA GPU attestation** to **Required** for a workload with a GPU. Click **ADD BUILD**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Check the build"
  image={require('../img/ccm-build-pending.webp').default}
  width={1568}
  height={665}
  marks={[[14.3, 61.6, 83.6, 7.0]]}
  alt="The application page with the new build listed, its approval pending">

The build is listed with its approval **Pending**. It is a request on the **Tasks** page.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
