<Walkthrough label="Add the application">
<Step
  title="Choose the type and platform"
  image={props.runtime === 'eos' ? require('../img/ccm-app-type-eos.webp').default : require('../img/ccm-app-type-coco.webp').default}
  width={1313}
  height={806}
  marks={props.runtime === 'eos' ? [[2.7, 18.1, 26.4, 18.2], [56.3, 65.3, 26.4, 18.2], [11.5, 88.7, 7.1, 6.5]] : [[29.5, 37.0, 26.4, 18.2], [29.5, 65.3, 26.4, 18.2], [11.5, 88.7, 7.1, 6.5]]}
  alt="The Add Application dialog, with the application type and Intel TDX selected">

Go to **Applications** and click **ADD APPLICATION**. Select {props.runtime === 'eos' ? <strong>Confidential Container - Enclave OS</strong> : <strong>Confidential Container - CoCo</strong>}, then the platform, such as **Intel TDX**, and click **NEXT**.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Enter the details"
  image={props.runtime === 'eos' ? require('../img/ccm-app-details-eos.webp').default : require('../img/ccm-app-details.webp').default}
  width={1293}
  height={839}
  marks={props.runtime === 'eos' ? [[2.7, 25.9, 44.4, 9.2], [2.7, 39.6, 44.4, 9.3]] : [[2.7, 25.9, 44.4, 9.2], [2.7, 39.6, 44.4, 9.3]]}
  alt="The application details, with the name and the group outlined">

Enter the **Application name**, such as <code>{props.name ?? 'llama-chat'}</code>, a **Description** if you like, and select the **Group**, such as <code>{props.group ?? 'llm-serving'}</code>.

</Step>
<Step
  title="Add the certificate domain"
  image={require('../img/ccm-app-certificate.webp').default}
  width={1293}
  height={521}
  marks={[[2.7, 15.9, 44.4, 15.9], [19.1, 91.4, 15.7, 8.6]]}
  alt="The certificate configuration, with the domain and the ADD APPLICATION button outlined">

Under **Certificate Configuration**, click **ADD CERTIFICATE** and enter the **Domain** of the workload's certificate, such as <code>{props.domain ?? 'chat.northwind.example'}</code>. Leave **Type** as **Certificate Issued by Confidential Computing Manager**, and click **ADD APPLICATION**.

</Step>
</Walkthrough>
