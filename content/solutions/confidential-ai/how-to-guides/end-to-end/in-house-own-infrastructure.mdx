Your organization owns the model and the infrastructure. One CCM account and one DSM account are enough, and there is no shared workflow. Give CCM approvals and DSM keys to a team other than the one that runs the infrastructure.

## Before you begin

- [Plan the deployment](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment), and choose the runtime, Confidential Containers or Enclave OS. Each linked task has both.
- Register the workload in the same CCM account the infrastructure is enrolled in. The zone CA you upload to DSM is your own.
