Your organization owns the model and the GPUs. One CCM account and one DSM account are enough, and there is no shared workflow. Give CCM approvals and DSM keys to a team other than the platform team.

## Who is involved

<RolesExplorer scenarios={["inhouse-own"]} />

## Before you begin

- [Plan the deployment](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment), and choose the runtime, Confidential Containers or Enclave OS. Each linked step has both.
- Register the workload in the same CCM account the nodes are enrolled in. You don't need a shared workflow, and the zone CA you upload to DSM is your own.

## Steps

Each step links to its task in the how-to guides; do them in this order.

| | Day | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Application team | [Set up CCM: create a group, a join token and the zone CA](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-gpu-nodes#set-up-ccm) |

| 4 | 0 | Platform team | [Install the runtime](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-gpu-nodes#install-the-runtime) |

## Verify

The consumer's last checks confirm the whole chain: CCM's audit log shows a successful `REQUEST_APP_CERTIFICATE`, the build is deployed, and the DSM Trusted CA app shows the key being used.
