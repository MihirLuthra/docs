Your organization runs its own model on rented servers. You are the model owner and the model consumer, and the cloud or NCP only prepares and enrols the servers. Key release is required, so the provider only ever stores ciphertext.

- Register the workload in the same CCM account the servers are enrolled in. The zone CA you upload to DSM is your own.
