Your organization runs its own model on rented infrastructure. You are the model owner and the model consumer, and the cloud or NCP only prepares and enrolls the infrastructure. Key release is required, so the provider only ever stores ciphertext.

- Register the workload in the same CCM account the infrastructure is enrolled in. The zone CA you upload to DSM is your own.
