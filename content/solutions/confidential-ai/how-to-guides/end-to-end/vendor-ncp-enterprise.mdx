A model vendor's model runs on an NCP's GPUs, and an enterprise uses it. Three companies each do their part.

## Who is involved

<RolesExplorer scenarios={["vendor-ncp-enterprise"]} />

## Before you begin

- [Plan the deployment](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment), and choose the runtime, Confidential Containers or Enclave OS. Each linked step has both.
- The model owner and the consumer use separate CCM accounts, linked by a shared workflow. The owner approves it first.

## Steps

Each step links to its task in the how-to guides; do them in this order.

| | Day | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Enterprise | [Set up CCM: create a group, a join token and the zone CA](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-gpu-nodes#set-up-ccm) |
| 2 | 0 | NCP | [Prepare the host](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-gpu-nodes#prepare-the-host) |
| 3 | 0 | NCP | [Enrol the node with the join token](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-gpu-nodes#enrol-the-node) |
| 4 | 0 | NCP | [Install the runtime](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-gpu-nodes#install-the-runtime) |
| 5 | 0 | Model vendor | [Set up DSM with the zone CA](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/set-up-dsm) |

## Verify

The consumer's last checks confirm the whole chain: CCM's audit log shows a successful `REQUEST_APP_CERTIFICATE`, the build is deployed, and the DSM Trusted CA app shows the key being used.
