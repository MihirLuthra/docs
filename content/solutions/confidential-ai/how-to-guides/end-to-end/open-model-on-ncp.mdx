## Before you begin

- [Plan the deployment](/solutions/confidential-ai/how-to-guides/day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment), and choose the runtime, Confidential Containers or Enclave OS. Each linked task has both.
- Skip the model owner's DSM and encryption steps. If the workload needs keys for other data, for example encrypted documents for retrieval, set up DSM the same way and keep those keys there.
- Register the workload in the same CCM account the servers are enrolled in. If you use DSM, upload your own zone CA to it.
