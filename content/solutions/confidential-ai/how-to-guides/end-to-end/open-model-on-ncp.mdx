## Before you begin

- Choose the runtime, Confidential Containers or Enclave OS. Each linked step has both.
- Skip the model owner's DSM and encryption steps. If the workload needs keys for other data, for example encrypted documents for retrieval, set up DSM the same way and keep those keys there.
- Register the workload in the same CCM account the nodes are enrolled in. You don't need a shared workflow. If you use DSM, upload your own zone CA to it.

## Verify

The consumer's last checks confirm the whole chain: CCM's audit log shows a successful `REQUEST_APP_CERTIFICATE`, the build is deployed, and clients that trust the zone CA can connect to the workload.
