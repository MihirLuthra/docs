As the model consumer, you run the model and send it your data. You own the CCM account the infrastructure belongs to, run the workload, and check that it really runs in a TEE before you trust it with anything.

## Your tasks

### Day 0 · Plan and prepare

1. [Plan the deployment](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment.mdx) with the model owner.
2. [Set up CCM](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/set-up-ccm.mdx#model-consumer). Create a group and share the zone CA with the model owner.
3. [Get the join token](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-the-infrastructure.mdx#get-the-join-token) and give it to the infrastructure provider.

### Day 1 · Deploy

1. [Share the model](../day-1-deploy/share-the-model.mdx) through a shared workflow, as the consumer.
2. [Deploy and verify](../day-1-deploy/deploy-and-verify.mdx), then connect clients.

### Day 2 · Operate

**Update the model**

1. [Approve the new workflow version](../day-2-operate/update-the-model.mdx#update-the-shared-workflow), when the model is shared.
2. [Redeploy](../day-2-operate/update-the-model.mdx#redeploy) with the new version.

**Audit and revoke access**

1. [Audit and revoke access](../day-2-operate/audit-and-revoke-access.mdx).
