As the model owner, you make sure only your approved workload, inside a TEE, can ever read the weights. You keep the key in DSM, encrypt the model, and register its measurements in CCM. The model consumer then runs it.

## Your tasks

### Day 0 · Plan and prepare

1. [Plan the deployment](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment.mdx) with the consumer.
2. [Set up CCM](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/set-up-ccm.mdx#model-owner). Add the application and note its application ID.
3. [Set up DSM](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/set-up-dsm.mdx). Create the encryption app, and the Trusted CA app with the consumer's zone CA.
4. [Protect the model](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/protect-the-model.mdx). Encrypt it, take measurements, add and approve the build.

### Day 1 · Deploy

1. [Share the model](../day-1-deploy/share-the-model.mdx) through a shared workflow, as the publisher. Skip this when you also run the model.

### Day 2 · Operate

**Update the model**

1. [Build the new version](../day-2-operate/update-the-model.mdx#build-the-new-version).
2. [Add and approve it as a new build](../day-2-operate/update-the-model.mdx#register-and-approve-the-build).
3. [Approve the new workflow version](../day-2-operate/update-the-model.mdx#update-the-shared-workflow), when the model is shared.

**Update the workload TCB**

1. [Take measurements again](../day-2-operate/update-the-workload-tcb.mdx#take-measurements-again) for the new runtime release.
2. [Approve the new build before the upgrade](../day-2-operate/update-the-workload-tcb.mdx#approve-before-the-upgrade).

**Audit and revoke access**

1. [Audit and revoke access](../day-2-operate/audit-and-revoke-access.mdx).
