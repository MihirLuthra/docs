As the model owner, you make sure the weights are only ever readable by your approved workload inside a TEE. You keep the key in DSM, encrypt the model, build the confidential image, and register its measurements in CCM. The model consumer then runs it.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 0 | [Plan the deployment](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment.mdx) with the consumer |
| 0 | [Set up DSM](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/set-up-dsm.mdx) with the consumer's zone CA |
| 0 | [Protect the model](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/protect-the-model.mdx): encrypt, build and measure, register and approve |
| 1 | [Share the model](../day-1-deploy/share-the-model.mdx) through a shared workflow, as the publisher |
| 2 | [Update the model or the runtime](../day-2-operate/update-the-model-or-runtime.mdx) |
| 2 | [Audit and revoke access](../day-2-operate/audit-and-revoke-access.mdx) |

The [end-to-end guides](../end-to-end/vendor-to-customer.mdx) show where these fit among the other roles' tasks.
