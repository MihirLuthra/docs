## Verify attestation

1. In CCM, open the application. The audit log shows a successful `REQUEST_APP_CERTIFICATE` event.
2. For Confidential Containers, the **Certificates** tab shows the issued certificate, and on **Builds** the build is **Deployed**.
3. The model owner sees the key used in the activity log of the DSM Trusted CA app.

## Connect clients

The workload's CCM certificate is a TLS certificate for its approved domain, such as `chat.northwind.example`, issued by your CCM account.

Whether a client can rely on it depends on who the client is.

- **Clients that trust your zone CA**, such as applications and users inside your organization that care about attestation. You give them your zone CA certificate. A client that trusts it, and connects to that domain, knows it reached the attested workload.
- **End users outside your organization** can't trust your zone CA, so the certificate proves nothing to them about attestation. Treat their TLS connection as ordinary TLS.
