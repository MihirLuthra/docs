The model consumer does this. Check attestation before you send the model any data.

## Before you begin

- The infrastructure is [prepared](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-the-infrastructure.mdx).
- The model's build is approved, and, for another owner's model, the [shared workflow](share-the-model.mdx) is approved.
- The runtime you [chose](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/plan-a-deployment.mdx#choose-the-runtime). Every tab set on this page follows your choice.

</Tab>

## Verify attestation

1. In CCM, open the application. The audit log shows a successful `REQUEST_APP_CERTIFICATE` event.
2. For Confidential Containers, the **Certificates** tab shows the issued certificate, and on **Builds** the build is **Deployed**.
3. The model owner sees the key used in the activity log of the DSM Trusted CA app.

## Connect clients

The workload's CCM certificate is a TLS certificate for its approved domain, such as `chat.northwind.example`, signed by your zone CA. A client that trusts your zone CA, and connects to that domain, knows it reached the attested workload.
