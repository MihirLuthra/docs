Skip this when one organization both owns and runs the model. Otherwise the consumer runs the owner's application through a shared workflow between their two CCM accounts. The model owner is the **publisher**, the model consumer the **consumer**.

## Before you begin

- The model owner has [protected the model](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/protect-the-model.mdx), and its build is approved.
- The consumer has a group in its CCM account, from [Set up CCM](../day-0-plan-and-prepare/prepare-the-servers.mdx#set-up-ccm).

## Link the accounts and approve the workflow

Follow [Run another account's application in a shared workflow](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/workflows/shared-workflows). In short, these are the steps.

1. The owner generates a collaboration token and sends it to the consumer, who accepts it. The owner then accepts the link.
2. The consumer creates a workflow with a placeholder for the owner's group.
3. The owner fills the placeholder with the model's application image.
4. The consumer requests approval. The owner approves first, then the consumer.
