## Audit

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| CCM, the application's audit log | Every certificate request, as `REQUEST_APP_CERTIFICATE` events |
| CCM, the application's **Builds** tab | Which builds are **Deployed** |
| CCM, **Tasks** | Requests that are **Pending**, **Approved**, **Declined** or **Failed** |
| CCM, **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES** | Each node's status, such as **Provisioned** or **Offline**, and when it last attested |
| DSM, the Trusted CA app's activity log | Each use of the model's key |

Check these after every deployment and update, and whenever a workload restarts unexpectedly.

## Revoke access

| To stop | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| A consumer running your model | Model owner or consumer | In CCM, **COLLABORATION GROUPS**, **REVOKE**. The shared workflow stops. |
| A build or domain that isn't approved yet | Approver | On **Tasks**, **DECLINE** the request. |
| The model's key being released | Model owner | In DSM, on the Trusted CA app |

Changing CCM's label policy doesn't revoke certificates already issued. It applies only when a certificate is next requested.
