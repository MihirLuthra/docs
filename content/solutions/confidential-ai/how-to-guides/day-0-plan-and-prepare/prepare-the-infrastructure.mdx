The model runs on infrastructure of TEE hosts, with CPUs and NVIDIA GPUs in confidential computing mode. The model consumer creates the join token, and the infrastructure provider uses it to enroll the hosts. When one organization is both, one team can do it all.

## Before you begin

- Hosts with a supported TEE, listed in the [support matrix](../../support-matrix.mdx), and NVIDIA GPUs that support confidential computing. Check the GPU, VBIOS and driver combination in the [NVIDIA Secure AI compatibility matrix](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/solutions/confidential-computing/secure-ai-compatibility-matrix/).
- The runtime you [chose](plan-a-deployment.mdx#choose-the-runtime). Every tab set on this page follows your choice.

## Set up CCM

The model consumer does this, in its Armor account.

Create a group for the deployment, such as `llm-serving`, or use one you already have.

<CreateGroup group="llm-serving" />

Get the join token and the zone CA. Give the join token to the infrastructure provider, who enrolls the hosts with it, and the zone CA certificate to the model owner, whose DSM app uses it to trust your workload's certificate.

<JoinToken />

## Prepare the hosts

The infrastructure provider does this on every host.

1. Follow NVIDIA's deployment guide for confidential computing for the hardware, BIOS, host software and host validation.
2. [Prepare the host for CCM](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enroll-an-intel-tdx-node#prepare-the-host). On Intel TDX, enable TDX, turn SMT off, and register the platform with Intel.

## Enroll the hosts

[Install the node agent](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enroll-an-intel-tdx-node#install-the-node-agent) with the consumer's join token. CCM calls an enrolled host a compute node. It appears in the consumer's CCM account under **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES**.

## Verify

- The hosts are listed in the consumer's CCM account under **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES**.
- For Confidential Containers, the `gpu-operator` pods and the `kata-as-coco-runtime` daemon set are running.
- For Enclave OS, every GPU shows `Kernel driver in use` as `vfio-pci`.
