The model consumer creates the join token; the infrastructure provider prepares the hosts with it. When they are the same organization, one team can do both.

## Before you begin

- Bare-metal hosts with Intel TDX CPUs and NVIDIA GPUs that support confidential computing. Check the GPU, VBIOS and driver combination in the [NVIDIA Secure AI compatibility matrix](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/solutions/confidential-computing/secure-ai-compatibility-matrix/).
- The runtime you [chose](plan-a-deployment.mdx#choose-the-runtime). Every tab set on this page follows your choice.

## Set up CCM

The model consumer does this.

1. In CCM, go to **Groups** and click **+ ADD GROUP**, or use an existing group, such as `llm-serving`. Enter a **Name** and click **SAVE**.
2. [Get a join token](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enrol-an-intel-tdx-node#get-a-join-token), and give it to the infrastructure provider.
3. In the same dialog, click **DOWNLOAD ZONE CA**, and give the certificate to the model owner. Their DSM app uses it to trust your workload's certificate.

## Prepare the host

The infrastructure provider does this on every host.

1. Follow NVIDIA's deployment guide for confidential computing on Intel TDX for the hardware, BIOS, host software and host validation.
2. [Prepare the host for CCM](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enrol-an-intel-tdx-node#prepare-the-host): enable Intel TDX, disable SMT, and register the platform with Intel.

## Enrol the node

[Install the node agent](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enrol-an-intel-tdx-node#install-the-node-agent) with the consumer's join token. The node then appears in the consumer's CCM account under **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES** > **Intel TDX**.

## Verify

- The nodes are listed in the consumer's CCM account under **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES** > **Intel TDX**.
- For Confidential Containers, the `gpu-operator` pods and the `kata-as-coco-runtime` daemon set are running.
- For Enclave OS, every GPU shows `Kernel driver in use: vfio-pci`.
