The model runs on servers with Intel TDX CPUs and NVIDIA GPUs in confidential computing mode. The model consumer creates the join token, and the infrastructure provider uses it to enrol the servers. When one organization is both, one team can do it all.

## Before you begin

- Servers with Intel TDX CPUs and NVIDIA GPUs that support confidential computing. Check the GPU, VBIOS and driver combination in the [NVIDIA Secure AI compatibility matrix](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/solutions/confidential-computing/secure-ai-compatibility-matrix/).
- The runtime you [chose](plan-a-deployment.mdx#choose-the-runtime). Every tab set on this page follows your choice.

## Set up CCM

The model consumer does this.

1. In CCM, go to **Groups** and click **+ ADD GROUP**, or use an existing group, such as `llm-serving`. Enter a **Name** and click **SAVE**.
2. [Get a join token](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enrol-an-intel-tdx-node#get-a-join-token), and give it to the infrastructure provider.
3. In the same dialog, click **DOWNLOAD ZONE CA**, and give the certificate to the model owner. Their DSM app uses it to trust your workload's certificate.

## Prepare the servers

The infrastructure provider does this on every server.

1. Follow NVIDIA's deployment guide for confidential computing on Intel TDX for the hardware, BIOS, host software and host validation.
2. [Prepare the server for CCM](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enrol-an-intel-tdx-node#prepare-the-host). Enable Intel TDX, turn SMT off, and register the platform with Intel.

## Enrol the servers

[Install the node agent](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/compute-nodes/enrol-an-intel-tdx-node#install-the-node-agent) with the consumer's join token. CCM calls an enrolled server a compute node. It appears in the consumer's CCM account under **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES** > **Intel TDX**.

## Verify

- The servers are listed in the consumer's CCM account under **Infrastructure** > **COMPUTE NODES** > **Intel TDX**.
- For Confidential Containers, the `gpu-operator` pods and the `kata-as-coco-runtime` daemon set are running.
- For Enclave OS, every GPU shows `Kernel driver in use` as `vfio-pci`.
