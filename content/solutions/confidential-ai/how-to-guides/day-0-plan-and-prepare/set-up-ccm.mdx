Each party with a CCM account does its part here, in its own Armor account. When one organization both owns and runs the model, it does both parts in one account.

## Model owner

### Create a group

Create a group for the model's application, such as `llm-serving`, or use one you already have.

<CreateGroup group="llm-serving" />

### Add the application

[Add the application](/products/fortanix-armor/confidential-computing/ccm/applications/register-an-application#add-the-application) `llama-chat` in the group, with the domain of its certificate, such as `chat.northwind.example`.

Note its **application ID**, the UUID at the end of the application's address in CCM (`…/applications/<APPLICATION_ID>`). The DSM app that releases the model key expects the DNS name `<APPLICATION_ID>.app.fortanix.cloud`, which CCM puts in the workload's certificate only after verifying its attestation.

You add builds later, once you have the measurements, in [Protect the model](protect-the-model.mdx).

## Model consumer

### Create the consumer's group

In its own Armor account, the consumer creates a group for the deployment, such as `llm-serving`.

<CreateGroup group="llm-serving" />

### Share the zone CA

Download the zone CA certificate and give it to the model owner, whose DSM app uses it to trust the workload's certificate.

<ZoneCa joinToken={false} />
