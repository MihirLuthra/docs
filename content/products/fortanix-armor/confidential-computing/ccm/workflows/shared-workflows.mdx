A shared workflow lets one CCM account run an application that another account owns, without the owner handing over the application. The **publisher** owns the application, and the **consumer** runs it on its own infrastructure. The two link their groups as collaborating groups, and both approve the workflow.

Collaborating groups link two accounts. Within one account, [create a workflow](create-a-workflow.mdx).

## Before you begin

- Each side has its own CCM account and a group.
- The publisher has [registered the application](../applications/register-an-application.mdx), its build is approved, and it has an [application configuration](../concepts.md#application-configurations) for the build.

## Change or end the collaboration

- An approved workflow can't be edited. **EDIT AS DRAFT** creates a new version, which replaces the old one once both sides approve it.
- Either side can end the collaboration from **COLLABORATION GROUPS** with **REVOKE**. The workflow then stops.

## Verify

- The workflow is listed under **Workflows** > **Approved**.
